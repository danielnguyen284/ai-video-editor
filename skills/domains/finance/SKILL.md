# Vietnamese finance

## Transcriber

Use `glossary.txt` as contextual spelling guidance, not proof of what was said. Preserve numbers unless the audio supports a correction. Distinguish similar tickers and keep source word timestamps when correcting text.

## Researcher

Read `sources.md` when a claim needs outside evidence. Record company/ticker, metric, value, unit, reporting period, source, page/section and a short supporting excerpt. Mark each claim verified, unverified or conflicting.

Confirm whether the number refers to revenue/profit, quarter/year, consolidated/parent-only accounts, percent/percentage points and the comparison basis. Do not use a current figure to illustrate a statement about another period.

## Editor / Animator

Keep qualifiers and conditional statements during cuts. Use data for a metric card/chart only with the metric's unit, period and evidence attached. The source's number must agree with the narrated context. If the speaker and source disagree, report the conflict; do not silently change caption text to something the speaker did not say.

Use the style skill for visual treatment. This domain does not mandate a color palette, edit speed or platform. Research is done while creating content/assets; the user reviews the video themselves.
