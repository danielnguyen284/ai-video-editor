# Source selection for finance research

Start with the document appropriate to the claim: company financial statements and investor-relations disclosures for company results; exchange/regulator publications for market announcements. Use secondary aggregators to locate or cross-check information, with the original document where available.

Capture the document title, publication date, reporting period, URL, page/section and the date accessed. A search-result snippet is not a substitute for reading the cited table or disclosure.

For each result, distinguish what the speaker said from what a source confirms. Store unverified/conflicting results explicitly. Researcher returns evidence to Editor; neither role silently fabricates missing figures.
